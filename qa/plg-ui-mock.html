How Insurance Companies and Lawyers Calculate Your Settlement

Insurance adjusters use automated software (such as Colossus or ClaimIQ), while trial lawyers evaluate jury verdict trends in King County, Pierce County, and Snohomish County courts. Both rely on the classical Multiplier Framework.

1

Total Economic Base

Tangible damages include past hospital bills, future physical therapy, documented lost income, and out-of-pocket medical devices.

2

The Multiplier Band

Pain, emotional distress, and disruption to daily lifestyle are represented by a severity multiplier, generally ranging from 1.5x up to 5.0x+.

3

Adjust for Case Levers

Pre-existing conditions, gaps in treatment, police citations, witness credibility, and venue demographics push the multiplier up or down.

4

Apply Policy Reality

Insurance carriers cannot pay beyond bodily injury limits without bad faith. Identifying underinsured motorist (UIM) coverage is essential.

Formula Architectures

Demand Formula vs Adjuster Formula

Plaintiff Demand Model: Gross = [(Medical Specials + Lost Wages + OOP) × Multiplier] + Property Damage
Insurance Adjuster Model: Gross = (Medical Specials × Multiplier) + Lost Wages + OOP + Property Damage

Straightforward answers from experienced Washington personal injury attorneys.

Washington is a "pure comparative negligence" state. Under RCW § 4.22.005, your recovery is reduced by your exact percentage of responsibility, but you are never barred from recovering compensation. For example, if your total damages are $100,000 and you are determined to have been 25% at fault, you can still collect $75,000 from the other driver. Even if you are 80% at fault, you may collect 20%.
No. Washington is one of the strongest pro-consumer states in the nation. In the landmark case Sofie v. Fibreboard Corp., the Washington Supreme Court ruled that statutory caps on non-economic damages (pain and suffering) violate the constitutional right to a trial by jury. There is no artificial government cap on what a Washington jury can award you.
Under RCW § 4.16.080, Washington has a three-year statute of limitations from the date of the collision to file a formal lawsuit. If a government entity or transit vehicle (like King County Metro or Sound Transit) was involved, earlier administrative tort claims notices are mandatory.
Washington requires only $25,000 per person in bodily injury liability, which is quickly depleted after a brief ER visit. When this occurs, Premier Law Group investigates your own auto insurance policy for Underinsured Motorist (UIM) and Personal Injury Protection (PIP) benefits, which step in to pay the remainder of your fair settlement.
